The Estrogen Clock

Your biology now, read against the years since your final period.

Two women can both be 50 and be in completely different places. One is still having periods. The other had her last one five years ago. Her heart, bones and metabolism change on that second clock, not on her birthday.

BiomarkHER shows where you are on each system, so you know what’s coming and what is worth doing now, while it can still make a difference.

Your system scores

Every result comes with the reason behind it.

Each part of your body gets its own score, never one number for all of you. Open any score and see which result moved it, and what estrogen loss has to do with it.

Taking a statin, HRT or a supplement? See what each one is doing to your results over time, and hear plainly when the research in women shows nothing.
